# S1.4 / P3 — historical reference families: what can be validated, and on what

Retrospective by construction. This session has read the twenty corpus families, so nothing
here is a blind test and no result may be reported as one. The families are used **after** the
run, as references — never as inputs to discovery.

## The first finding is about the reference set, not the engine

None of the three named reference families is present in the only kernel image available
offline on this machine (PS4 FW 13.52, `kernel.bin` SHA-256 `cf157ffd…`):

| family | class | affected range (corpus) | fixed in | present in 13.52? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `lapse-aio-multi-delete` | DOUBLE_FREE | 5.00 – 12.02 | 12.50 | **no** |
| `netcontrol-double-fdrop` | REFCOUNT | 12.00 – 13.00 | 12.02 PS5 / 13.02 PS4 | **no** |
| `relapse-aio-multi-wait` | UAF | 7.00 – 11.00 (PS5), … up to 13.60 | 14.00 | **no** (and the case is PS5) |

So on this image the three cannot be positive rediscovery targets at all. Treated as positives
they would generate exactly one kind of result: a false negative that says "the engine missed
Lapse", when the honest statement is "the engine was pointed at a firmware where Lapse does not
exist". They are usable as **negative controls**: the engine must *not* report a double free at
`aio_multi_delete` on 13.52.

## Why even the negative control is not decidable here

A negative control needs to bind a finding to the family's function. Two inventories exist and
neither can do it:

* the engine's own scan has no symbols, only RVAs;
* `/home/hermes/ps4-r2-pipeline/db/ps4r2-final.db` holds 16 811 functions, but every name is
  `FUN_<va>` — zero semantic names — and a lookup for `aio_multi`, `netevent` or
  `aio_multi_delete` returns nothing.

Without at least one of (a) a symbol table for 13.52, (b) an image inside an affected range, or
(c) a per-family site recorded as an oracle, the family-level statement cannot be made. That is
the concrete missing semantics for P3, and it is a data problem, not a detector problem.

## What the engine can close by itself, and at which rung

Measured on FW 13.52 (see `S1.4-MEASUREMENT.md` for the numbers and the artefacts):

* **Dispatch** — 687 syscall slots resolved out of `sysent`, each with the table slot and the
  pointer that was read, converted VA → RVA through the image base. This is the
  "userland → kernel" part of the derivation and it is fully mechanical.
* **CFG reachability** — every chain that reaches a resolved entry lands on `CFG_REACHABLE`
  with every hop witnessed.
* **Object identity (intra-function)** — alias-tracked, field-offset-backed, and refused when
  the object is passed in an argument register but the callee does not use it.
* **Primitive preconditions** — modelled per bug class; the offline-decidable part closes,
  the live part (spray, second context, widening, stability) never does.

What it cannot close, and the reason:

1. `REQ_UNPRIV_ENTRY` — no authorization symbol is resolved in this firmware, and the cdevsw
   record carries no mode field. Every sampled entry stayed `UNKNOWN` with that obligation
   recorded.
2. `REQ_OBJECT_FLOW` across more than one call — needs per-function summaries of what a callee
   does with its argument; `transfers-reference` edges exist only where argument provenance is
   proved, and the rest stay `unknown` rather than being assumed.
3. `REQ_CHAIN_STABILITY` — needs hardware. `EXPLOIT_CHAIN_VALIDATED` is unreachable offline by
   construction, which is the whole point of the ladder.

## What this means for the next real blind test

The order of work is now dictated by evidence rather than by ambition:

1. **Resolve authorization symbols** (or add a device-record mode table). Without it no chain
   can pass rung 2, so no object-flow result can be *used*, and a holdback case would be scored
   at `CFG_REACHABLE` no matter how good the analysis is.
2. **Pick a holdback family whose affected range includes an image you actually have**, or
   obtain an image inside the affected range of a chosen family. The three named families
   cannot serve it on 13.52.
3. **Add a family-level oracle** (site per case) so a negative control is decidable without
   symbols.
