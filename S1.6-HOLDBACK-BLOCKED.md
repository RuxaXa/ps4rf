# S1.6 P4 — Holdback evaluation: **BLOCKED**

The P4 acceptance criterion asks for at least one independently held-out historical bug case from a
**different family**, with rules, search space and ranking frozen *before* the case was known.

## Verdict

**BLOCKED.** No independent holdback case exists in the current corpus. Nothing was promoted to a
blind case, and no known or contaminated case was re-labelled as one.

## Evidence

From the read-only corpus/holdback review (`deleg_646c8925`, task 3) and re-checked against the
tree:

* `splits.verify()` reports `blind_claim: False` for the current evaluation root; the train split
  cannot carry a blind claim by construction.
* `splits.assert_blind()` refuses with `SplitError: kind 'train' can never carry a blind claim`.
  That refusal is the correct behaviour, not a gap to work around.
* The oracle entries (`solutions/`, `training/references.json`) carry `bug_class`, `case_id`,
  `discovery_approach`, `firmware`, `role` and `root_cause`, but **no `site` and no `address`**, and
  `image_sha256` is `null` for all 19 families. Without a site there is nothing to hold out: a
  hit could not be distinguished from a miss.
* `tools/discovery/data/families.json` contains a leak that is still live in the tree: the family
  whose site the detector rules were derived with is described in the same file the rules are
  ranked from. Git history shows it was never reverted.
* All 19 corpus families are `training` (15) or `burned` (4). Zero families are eligible as
  holdback.

## Why the netcontrol differential does not substitute for it

FW 9.00 (pre-fix) versus FW 13.52 (post-fix) is a genuine differential over a published fix, and
S1.6 ran it (see `S1.6-MEASUREMENT.md`). It is **not** a blind case: the defect, the family and the
fix versions are known to us before the run, and the site was located using the public command
constants. Calling it a blind test would be exactly the re-labelling the S1.6 brief forbids.

## What would unblock it

1. A new family outside every commit, session and skill in this repository — i.e. a firmware or
   subsystem whose defect description is fetched *after* the rules, search space and ranking are
   hash-frozen.
2. An oracle with an explicit **site list** (`sites + bug_class`), not just a class.
3. An `image_sha256` for the evaluated image.
4. `splits.assert_blind()` passing on that split, verified before the run, not after.

Until then P4 stays BLOCKED and the overall S1.6 verdict cannot be PASS.
