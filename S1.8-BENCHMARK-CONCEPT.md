# Concept: an unburned PS4/PS5 rediscovery benchmark

Separate from S1.8. Nothing here reads a solution: the labels are produced by a mechanical procedure,
and the holdout stays sealed until a measurement is committed. No hardware, no console contact, no
network access during a run.

## Why the current corpus cannot serve as a benchmark

`relapse` is burned: its root cause was used as a training reference (documented in
`S1.5-RELAPSE-CORRECTION.md`), and its oracle carries no `site`/`address`, so `blind_claim` is false.
Every case we have looked at is either training or contamination. A benchmark needs cases whose
answers were never in front of us — not cases we promise not to look at.

## Label generation without reading solutions

The ground truth is a **version differential**, not an advisory text:

1. Pick two firmware versions of the same platform where both images are obtainable offline.
2. Compute the *change set* mechanically: the functions whose release/refcount handling differs,
   identified by structural comparison (same call graph position, different guard, different reset,
   different error path) — not by reading a write-up.
3. A case is a triple `(old_function, new_function, structural_delta)`. The label is the location
   and the delta class; the root cause stays unread until evaluation.
4. Every label needs **two independent evidence lines** before it may be used: the structural delta,
   and a second line (a call-site that becomes guarded, a reset that appears, a path that stops
   being reachable). A label with one line is stored as provisional and cannot enter the holdout.

This keeps the benchmark honest with zero knowledge of exploitability: the engine must find the
location *and* produce a defeasible proof; whether a case is exploitable is not the label.

## Partition (frozen before any run)

| Split | Size | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| calibration | 8 cases | may be inspected; used only to calibrate thresholds |
| validation | 6 cases | labels known, rule set frozen by hash before the run, one look allowed |
| holdout | 6 cases | envelopes sealed; opened only after the measurement is committed |

* The envelope is a file holding only: platform, both version strings, both image SHA-256 values, the
  function RVAs, the delta class, and the two evidence lines. It is hashed (`envelope.sha256`) and the
  hash is committed **before** the run.
* The holdout envelopes are not opened by whoever tunes the rules. A second party (or a scheduled
  job that only compares hashes) performs the comparison at evaluation time.
* Categories are stratified: refcount/ownership, table-slot lifecycle, lock coverage, error-path
  lifetime — so a single tuned heuristic cannot carry the whole score.

## Metrics (no aggregate that hides the failure modes)

* **Location recall** on the holdout at a fixed rank threshold, reported per category.
* **Discrimination**: for each triple, does the engine rank the old (broken) function above its fixed
  counterpart? A rule that fires on both scores 0.5 precision, and that must be visible.
* **Proof level**: how many hits carry a must-strength witness versus a `may` witness — a hit with
  only `CONDITIONAL` evidence is not the same result as a `PROVEN` one and may not be pooled with it.
* **False-positive count at actionable level on the *fixed* side**, which is the number that decides
  whether the engine is usable at all.
* Every metric is reported with its search space (functions scanned, call sites, time budget).

## Anti-contamination controls

1. **Rule freeze**: a hash over the rule modules and data files, committed before the run. A run with
   a different hash is not the same benchmark and cannot be compared to a previous score.
2. **No oracle reachable**: the engine gets the image and the anchor inventory only. It may not read
   the envelope, the label file, or any advisory text.
3. **Image purity is recorded, not assumed**: the image hash and any patch sites go into the report.
   A modified image invalidates the case.
4. **One shot per split**: a validation run may be repeated only with a new, committed hypothesis and
   a note about what changed. Endless re-runs on a known split are just fitting.
5. **Failure is reported, not patched away**: a blocked split stays blocked in the final number.

## PS5

The same procedure applies — the differential needs two obtainable versions and the anchor inventory
(release/free primitives, table conventions) per build. Procurement of a second, internally
consistent PS5 kernel build offline is the open question and is not solved here. PS4 stays the
primary platform until that is answered.

## What this benchmark does not claim

It does not measure exploitability, weaponisability or impact. It measures one thing: can the engine
rediscover a *fixed location* with a defensible ownership proof, on cases whose answers it never saw —
and does it stay quiet on the fixed side.
